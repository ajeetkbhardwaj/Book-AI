# ML Systems Design

System Design - Gathering requirements, Understanding business domain, Design a viable and scalable solution

Software design : Data, Model and Code are always changing during the system's lifecycle

ML Systems

Phase-0 : A full solution onto paper before writing any single line code and gather all possible requirements of the project.

1. What is the business problem we wanted to solve ?
2. Is ML best fit to approach the problem ?
3. What kind of domain knowledge is necessary to proceed ?
4. How to acquire data for model ?
5. How to pre-process data and on what scale ?
6. How many people do we need ?
7. Do we have a already fixed timeline for this project ?
8. How and Where our model to be trained ?
9. How will the model to be deployed ?
10. How we can scale our systems as user base grows ?
11. How we will maintain our system ?
12. Is it an offline or online system ?
13. How will we test our system ?
14. Can we reproduce the same results ?
15. Can we repeat the lifecycle from scratch if needed ?
16. How can we evaluate the success of our systems ?

Above are the some questions related to the full lifecycle of the software development, needed to be answered as accurately as possible such that they can be alterable during process. Hence we can split the ML Systems Lifecycle into three parts 

1. Data
2. Model
3. DevOps

What is data engineering ?

In ML workflow, data engineering is the process of gathering data, exploring them and transforming them before they are feed into the model.

What is model engineering ?

In ML's lifecycle starts from research and developments and then ends with model deployments.

What is operation engineering ?

Our ML application must be highly scalable, reliable and always available.
